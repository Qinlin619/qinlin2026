The Challenge & Vision

Friend trips are great.
Organizing a friend trip? Less great.
Deciding where to go, explore, and keep everyone in the loop—can get frustrating, fast.

That’s why TripUp was created: an all-in-one platform for organizing seamless group travel. whether it’s a weekend getaway or a music festival abroad.

Users can collaboratively build itineraries. vote on decisions. and settle expenses—reducing planning friction and amplifying the fun.

02

Patterns

Spontaneous Decisions

Most choices happen on the fly. especially mid-trip. The app must support quick interactions.

Shared Ownership

Everyone wants input. Particularly on food and expenses. A collective voice is essential.

Low Friction

If something takes more than a few taps. users switch to WhatsApp. Intuitive logic is key.

Contextual Goal
Rapid Decisions via diverse displays.
03

Needs

Polls Over Chats

Users prefer quick polls rather than chats. as long as the experience is intuitive.

Expense Tracking

Easy ways to manage and settle group expenses via digital methods or in-app payments.

Real-time Feedback

When decisions are made. users expect instant updates in the shared itinerary.

User Priority
Interactive Status Sync and Payment.
